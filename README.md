# HadMad.github

HadMad — Hadron Physics in Madrid is a research group based at the Universidad Complutense de Madrid (UCM) working on the phenomenology of hadrons, with a particular focus on low-energy strong interactions.

Our research combines theoretical and phenomenological approaches to study hadron–hadron scattering, resonance properties, and the analytic structure of scattering amplitudes. Our work includes dispersive methods, chiral effective theories, unitarization techniques, and connections with experimental and lattice-QCD information.

This organization provides open scientific resources associated with our research, including scattering amplitudes and parametrizations, and experimental data and revised datasets.

Our aim is to make the results of our research reproducible, reusable, and accessible to the hadron-physics community.
